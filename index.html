Core English Tenses Reference

Review formulas, rules, time markers, and examples before or during your quiz.

Present Simple

Habits & Facts
(+) Subject + V1 (+ s/es for He/She/It)
(-) Subject + do/does + not + V1
(?) Do/Does + Subject + V1?

When to Use:

  • Daily routines, habits, and repeated actions.
  • Universal truths, scientific facts, and general states.
  • Fixed schedules and timetables (trains, flights, classes).
  • Stative verbs (know, believe, love, understand, want).
Time Markers: always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never, every day/week, on Mondays.
Example: "Water boils at 100°C." / "She drinks coffee every morning."

Present Continuous

Actions in Progress
(+) Subject + am/is/are + V-ing
(-) Subject + am/is/are + not + V-ing
(?) Am/Is/Are + Subject + V-ing?

When to Use:

  • Actions happening right at the moment of speaking.
  • Temporary situations happening around now (this week/month).
  • Definite personal plans or arrangements in the near future.
  • Changing or developing situations.
Time Markers: now, right now, at the moment, currently, Look!, Listen!, today, this week.
Example: "Listen! Somebody is playing the piano." / "They are buying a house next month."

Past Simple

Completed Past
(+) Subject + V2 (regular -ed / irregular)
(-) Subject + did + not + V1
(?) Did + Subject + V1?

When to Use:

  • Completed actions at a specific finished time in the past.
  • A series of consecutive completed actions in a story.
  • Past habits or states that are no longer true.
  • Short interrupting actions (often paired with when).
Time Markers: yesterday, last night/week/year, ... ago, in 1992, back in the 1990s, when.
Example: "She lost her keys yesterday." / "Columbus discovered America in 1492."

Past Continuous

Ongoing Past Action
(+) Subject + was/were + V-ing
(-) Subject + was/were + not + V-ing
(?) Was/Were + Subject + V-ing?

When to Use:

  • An action in progress at a specific exact moment in the past.
  • An ongoing background action interrupted by a Past Simple event.
  • Two parallel actions happening at the exact same time in the past.
Time Markers: while, as, when, at 10 PM last night, at midnight, all day yesterday.
Example: "When you called, I was cooking dinner." / "While she read, he was sleeping."

Future Simple (Will / Going to)

Decisions & Plans
Will: Subject + will (won't) + V1
Going to: Subject + am/is/are + going to + V1

When to Use:

  • Will: Spontaneous decisions made at the moment of speaking, promises, offers, and general predictions (I think...).
  • Be going to: Prior plans/intentions already decided, and predictions based on visible present evidence (e.g., dark clouds).
Time Markers: tomorrow, next week/year, in 2030, soon, I promise, I think, Look at...!
Example: "Look at those clouds! It is going to rain." / "I promise I will help you."

Future Continuous

Ongoing Future
(+) Subject + will + be + V-ing
(-) Subject + will + not (won't) + be + V-ing
(?) Will + Subject + be + V-ing?

When to Use:

  • An action that will be in progress at a specific point in time in the future.
  • Actions that are already scheduled or expected to happen as part of a routine in the future.
Time Markers: at 8 PM tomorrow, this time next week/year, between 2 and 4 PM tomorrow.
Example: "This time next week, I will be lying on a sunny beach in Hawaii."
